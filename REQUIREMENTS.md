# Vehicle Maintenance Application Requirements

## Overview
A Vehicle Maintenance Management Application that helps vehicle owners track maintenance activities, fuel consumption, service schedules, and vehicle health. It serves both individual vehicle owners and fleet management companies.

## Core Features

### 1. Fuel Tracking & Mileage Calculation
- **Record fuel fill-up details:**
  - Date
  - Fuel quantity (Liters)
  - Fuel cost
  - Current odometer reading
- **Automatically calculate:**
  - Mileage/Average (KM per Liter)
  - Cost per KM
  - Monthly fuel expenditure
- Generate fuel consumption trends and reports.

### 2. Service Management
- Maintain complete service history of the vehicle.
- **Track:**
  - Service date
  - Odometer reading at service
  - Service type
  - Service center details
  - Service cost
  - Invoices and documents
- View and analyze maintenance expenses over time.

### 3. Service Reminder Automation
- **Automatically create reminders based on:**
  - Next service date
  - Next service due KM
  - Manufacturer-recommended intervals
- **Send notifications via:**
  - Mobile push notifications
  - Email
  - SMS (optional)
- Allow users to reschedule or update service dates.

## Additional Recommended Features

### 4. Vehicle Health Dashboard
- **Display:**
  - Current mileage
  - Last service date
  - Upcoming maintenance activities
  - Fuel efficiency trends
  - Total ownership cost

### 5. Document Management
- **Store and access:**
  - RC documents
  - Insurance papers
  - Pollution certificates
  - Service invoices
  - Warranty documents

### 6. Insurance & PUC Reminders
- **Track expiry dates for:**
  - Insurance
  - Pollution Certificate (PUC)
  - Road Tax
  - Driving License
- Generate renewal reminders before expiry.

### 7. Spare Parts & Component Tracking
- **Monitor replacement schedules for:**
  - Engine oil
  - Oil filter
  - Air filter
  - Battery
  - Tires
  - Brake pads
  - Coolant
- Get reminders based on KM or duration.

### 8. Expense Management
- **Track all vehicle-related expenses:**
  - Fuel
  - Service
  - Repairs
  - Insurance
  - Toll charges
  - Parking fees
- Generate monthly and yearly expense reports.

### 9. Multi-Vehicle Support
- Manage multiple vehicles under a single account.
- Separate maintenance records and reports for each vehicle.

### 10. Repair & Breakdown Log
- Record unexpected repairs and breakdowns.
- Maintain repair history and associated costs.

### 11. Analytics & Reports
- Fuel efficiency trends.
- Service cost analysis.
- Annual maintenance expenses.
- Vehicle performance reports.
- Download reports in PDF or Excel format.

### 12. Location-Based Features
- Save favorite service centers.
- Locate nearby fuel stations and service centers.
- Maintain service provider contact information.

## User Roles
- Vehicle Owner
- Fleet Manager
- Driver
- Admin

## AI-Based Smart Alerts (Future Enhancement)
- Predict upcoming maintenance based on historical data.
- Detect unusual fuel consumption patterns.
- Recommend optimal service schedules.

## Key Dashboard Metrics
- Current Odometer Reading
- Average Mileage (KM/L)
- Fuel Cost per KM
- Total Fuel Expense
- Total Maintenance Expense
- Next Service Due Date
- Next Service Due KM
- Insurance Expiry
- PUC Expiry
- Battery Health Status
- Tire Replacement Due
